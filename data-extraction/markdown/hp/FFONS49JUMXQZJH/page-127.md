## Filling Segments of Pie Charts

The algorithms to fill slices of pie charts are much more complex be­ cause the areas are not rectangular. Software packages such as the Graphics Presentations Pacs for various HP desktop computers make it easy to draw pie charts with area fill. You may wish to purchase such software so you do not have to invest hours of programming time in order to create filled pie charts.
