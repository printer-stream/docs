## Enhancing the Plot

## What You'llLearn in This Chapter

Nowthat you can draw lines, you are ready to create your own plots. In this chapter you will learn how to enhance your plots by using HP-GL instructions to draw tick marks on axes or create grids, draw a symbol or character of your choice at each data point, and draw dashed or dotted lines. All these enhancements will make your data easier to interpret.

## HP-GLInstructions Covered

XT The X-TickInstruction

- YT The Y-TickInstruction
- TL The Tick Length Instruction

SM The Symbol Mode Instruction

LT The Line Type Instruction
