## Switching to RAM character printing

| ESC/P 2   | ESC/P   | 9-Pin ESC/P   |
|-----------|---------|---------------|

Once you store user-defined characters in the printer (using the ESC &amp; command), use the ESC % command to tell the printer to switch to RAM character printing. The format of the command is as follows:

| ESC%0   | Selects ROMcharacters                        |
|---------|----------------------------------------------|
| ESC%1   | Selects user-defined characters (RAM memory) |
