## C O N F I D E N T I A L

- ■ Selects the black-color density in two-color printing ( a = 118)
- This setting's value affects printing in black for two-color printing. Setting the 'paper width,' 'print density,' 'select paper setting' ( a = 3, 5, 116 ) can be changed by 'Memory switch setting mode' by the panel switch operation when the power supply is turned on.

|   ( nL + nH × 256) | The black-color density   |
|--------------------|---------------------------|
|                 70 | Light                     |
|                 85 | Medium                    |
|                100 | Dark                      |
