## C O N F I D E N T I A L

[Model-dependent variations] TM-T90 , TM-T70 , TM-L90

<!-- image -->

## TM-T90 , TM-T70

The amount of paper feed of the Japanese Kanji model can specify even 900 mm or less {35.5 inches}. When the amount of paper feed which exceeds the maximum value is specified, the paper feed of the maximum amount is executed.

## TM-L90

When the label paper or the black mark paper is selected in standard mode and a paper feed amount that exceeds the remainder print area of the label, the printer feeds the label to the next print starting position.

The amount of paper feed can specify up to 900 mm {35.4 inches}. When the amount of paper feed which exceeds the maximum value is specified, the paper feed of the maximum amount is executed.
