## TM-J2000/J2100 , TM-T90 , TM-T20 , TM-T88IV , TM-T88V , TM-T70

These printers support the graphics functions of GS ( L / GS 8 L .

It is recommended that GS ( L / GS 8 L be used.

Values of [Dot density] and [Maximum print area (dot)] are the same as the graphic function. (See Function 69 of GS ( L / GS 8 L .)
