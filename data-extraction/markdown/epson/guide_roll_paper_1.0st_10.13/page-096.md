## C O N F I D E N T I A L

## TM-U230 , TM-U220

This command must not be executed consecutively more than two times. Reverse direction paper feeding causes the following problems:

- ■ Paper feed pitch is incorrect.
- ■ Printer noise is louder than normal.
- ■ The paper may rub against the ribbon and become dirty.

In the reverse direction, the maximum paper feed amount is 8.467 mm {48/144 inch}. If the specified amount exceeds 8.467 mm {48/144 inch}, the printer only prints the data and does not feed the paper.
