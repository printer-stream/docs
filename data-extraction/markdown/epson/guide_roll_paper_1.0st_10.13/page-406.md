## C O N F I D E N T I A L

## TM-T90 , TM-T88IV , TM-T70 , TM-L90

When m = 1, the PAPER OUT LED indicator blinks during a macro waiting state. When m = 1, the FEED can be Paper feed button.

## TM-T20 , TM-T88V

When m = 1, the Paper LED indicator blinks during a macro waiting state. When m = 1, the Feed can be Paper feed button.
