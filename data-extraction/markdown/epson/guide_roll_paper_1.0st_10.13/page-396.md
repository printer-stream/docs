## C O N F I D E N T I A L

## TM-T20 , TM-T88IV , TM-T88V , TM-T70

With UPC-E ( m = 1, 66), 11 or 12 bytes can be used for the amount of data to process. Only capital letters (ASCII = A ~ D/Hexadecimal = 41H ~ 44H/Decimal = 65 ~ 68) and small letters (ASCII = a ~ d/Hexadecimal = 61H ~ 64H/Decimal = 97 ~ 100 ) can be used for the start/stop character with CODABAR ( m = 6, 71).

## TM-P60

With UPC-E ( m = 1, 66), 6, 7, 8, 11 or 12 bytes can be used for the amount of data to process. Only capital letters (ASCII = A ~ D/Hexadecimal = 41H ~ 44H/Decimal = 65 ~ 68) and small letters (ASCII = a ~ d/Hexadecimal = 61H ~ 64H/Decimal = 97 ~ 100 ) can be used for the start/stop character with CODABAR ( m = 6, 71).
