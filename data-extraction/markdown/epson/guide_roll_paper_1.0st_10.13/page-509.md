## TM-P60

The vertical and horizontal motion units are about 0.125 mm {1/203 inch}. This corresponds to 1 dot pitch.

The line width of this printer is as shown in the table below.

|   m1 | Line type                         | Line width   |
|------|-----------------------------------|--------------|
|    1 | Continuous line: Thin             | 2 dots       |
|    2 | Continuous line: Moderately thick | 4 dots       |
|    3 | Continuous line: Thick            | 8 dots       |
