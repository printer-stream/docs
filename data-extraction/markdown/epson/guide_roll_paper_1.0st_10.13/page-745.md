## TM-T70

- ■ Print density settings ( a = 5)
- The Japanese specification (paper width 80 mm)does not support "Print density level 12" and "Print density level 13."
- The Japanese specification (paper width 58 mm) does not support "Print density level 10" through "Print density level 13."
- ■ Print speed ( a = 6)
