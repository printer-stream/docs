SI

[Name]

Select upside-down printing

[Code]

ASCII

SI

Hex.

0F

Decimal

15

[Defined Area]

- - -

[Initial Value]

Upside-down cancelled Specifies upside-down printing This command is enabled only when at the top of the line. This command is enabled for following.

[Function]

Upside down and right-side up characters cannot both exist in the same line.

- ANK characters

- Kanji characters

- Bit images

- Logos

- Bar codes
