6-2-1 
Identifying Transmission Status
The status of commands is identifiable because those transmitted by this printer use a dedicated but value.  However, if using ASB, the three bytes after confirming 
the first ASB byte, excluding XOFF, are processed as ASB data.  Without this, it is not possible to identify statuses such as GS r (Send status) and statuses after the 
second byte of an ASB.
