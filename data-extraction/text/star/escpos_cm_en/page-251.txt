Function	
Sends the all kind of multibyte font in the printer.
Details	
Sends in the following format.
	
ESC GS ) I pL pH fn k1 k2 [multibyte font kind1, multibyte font kind2, ..... ] LF NUL
k1 and k2 indicate the number of transmission data bytes (k1 + k2 * 256) after the multibyte 
font types.
When there are multiple multibyte font types in the printer, they are delimited by commas 
(2Ch). 
	
The kind of multibyte font is sent as character string data.
Kind of multibyte fonts
Transmitting string
Japanese
KANJI JAPANESE
Simplified  Chinese GB2312
 CHINA GB2312
Simplified Chinese GB18030 
CHINA GB18030
Traditional Chinese BIG5 
TAIWAN BIG-5
Korean
KOREA C-5601C
          Exemple: 	 When “Simplified Chinese GB18030" and "Traditional Chinese BIG5 " are installed in the printer:
	
ESC GS ) I pL pH fn k1 k2 CHINA GB18030 , TAIWAN BIG-5 , LF NUL
	
(k1 = 29, k2 =0)
	
When the multibyte font is not installed in the printer, the following data is sent:
	
ESC GS ) I pL pH fn k1 k2 LF NUL
	
(k1 = 2, k2 =0)
