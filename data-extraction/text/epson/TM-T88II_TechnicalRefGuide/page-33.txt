To use USB model TM printers, the TM printer driver (EPSON OPOS ADK or advanced printer

following URLs:
For customers from North America, go to the following web site: http://pos.epson.com/
For customers from other countries, go to the following web site:

For details on EPSON OPOS ADK or advanced printer driver, refer to “Introducing the Control 
Methods” (page 3-1).
