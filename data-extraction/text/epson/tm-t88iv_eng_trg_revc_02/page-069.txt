Attaching/Removing the Connector Cover
Attaching the Connector Cover
1
Connect all the cables.
The connector cover has three possible cable exits: on the right, left, and back.
2
Position the two hooks on the connector cover so that they hook the 
holes on the printer case. Push the connector cover down until it clicks.
Removing the Connector Cover
Place the printer with its bottom up. While pushing the both sides of the connector cover toward 
inside, push it down to detach the two hooks on it from the printer case.
