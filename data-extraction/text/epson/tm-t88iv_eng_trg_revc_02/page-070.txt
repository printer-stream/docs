Removing Jammed Paper
1
Turn off the printer and press the cover open lever to open the roll paper 
cover.
2
Remove the jammed paper, reinstall the roll, and close the roll paper 
cover.
When the Roll Paper Cover Cannot be Opened
1
Open the cutter cover.
2
Turn the knob until you see a triangle in the opening.
This returns the cutter blade to the normal position. There is a label near the cutter to assist 
you.
CAUTION
Do not touch the thermal head (See"Cleaning the Thermal Head" on page 72.) 
because it can be very hot after printing.
