[Description]
Transmits the status of paper sensor(s) as 1 byte of data.
[Recommended Functions]
This command is supported only by some printer models but will not be supported by future models.
It is recommended to use GS r 1 to check the status and GS r for transmitting the paper sensor status. 
[Notes]
■See GS r for details on status transmission.
■The paper sensor status to be transmitted is as follows:
