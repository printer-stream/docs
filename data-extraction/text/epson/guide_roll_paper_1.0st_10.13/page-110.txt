Character configurations
Bit 0: 
Font 1 = Font A (12 × 24) 
Font 2 = Font B (9 × 17)
Each character’s baseline is as follows:
Font A (12 × 24): 21 dots from the top of a character.
Font B (9 × 17): 16 dots from the top of a character.
