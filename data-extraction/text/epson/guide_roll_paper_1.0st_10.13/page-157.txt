Program Example for all printers
Print Sample
PRINT #1, CHR$(&H1D);"B";CHR$(1); ← Select
PRINT #1, "AAAAA"; CHR$(&HA);
PRINT #1, CHR$(&H1D);"B";CHR$(0); ← Cancel
PRINT #1, "BBBBB"; CHR$(&HA);
BBBBB ← Normal printing
AAAAA ← White/black reverse printing
