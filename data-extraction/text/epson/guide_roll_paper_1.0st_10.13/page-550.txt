C O N F I D E N T I A L
Paper roll
Miscellaneous commands     GS z 0 (TM-L90 w/ Peeler)
 
Ver. 10.13
p. 550
■The printer automatically in the status of not waiting for the label to be removed when an operator 
removes the label at the peeling position. If the printer is in an area where the label peeling detector 
cannot detect paper due to direct sunlight, the operator needs to press the FEED button once after 
removing the label. Installing the printer in a place with no direct sunlight is recommended.
Program Example for all printers
PRINT #1, CHR$(&h1D);"z0";CHR$(0);CHR$(60) ← Sets the online recovery wait time to 30 sec.
