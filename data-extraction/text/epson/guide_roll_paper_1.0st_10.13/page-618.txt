[Description]
Selects the error correction level for QR Code.
[Notes]
■Settings of this function affect the processing of Functions 181 and 182.
■QR Code employs Reed-Solomon error correction to generate a series of error correction codewords.
■Settings of this function are effective until ESC @ is executed, the printer is reset, or the power is 
turned off.
