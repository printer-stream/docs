■Paper sensor status (n = 1, 49)
•
The roll paper near-end sensor is an option. If the printer is not equipped with the paper near-end sensor, 
bits 0 and 1 of the paper sensor status are always OFF “Paper adequate.”
•
When the roll paper end sensor detects a paper-end, the printer goes offline and does not execute this 
command. Therefore, bits 2 and 3 of the paper sensor status do not transmit a paper-end status.
