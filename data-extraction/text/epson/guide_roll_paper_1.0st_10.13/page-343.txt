■Roll paper sensor status (n = 4)
The roll paper near-end sensor is an option. If the printer is not equipped with the paper near-end 
sensor, bits 2 and 3 of the third byte are always ON, “Paper adequate.”
When the cover is open, the status of the roll paper end sensor (bit 5, 6) retain the value when the 
cover was closed immediately before.
