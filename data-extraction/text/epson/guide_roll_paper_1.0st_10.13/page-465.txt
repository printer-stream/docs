[Description]
Outputs the pulse specified by t1 and t2 to the specified connector pin m as follows: 
•
The pulse for ON time is (t1 × 2 msec) and for OFF time is (t2 × 2 msec).
[Notes]
■If t2 < t1, the OFF time is equal to the ON time.
