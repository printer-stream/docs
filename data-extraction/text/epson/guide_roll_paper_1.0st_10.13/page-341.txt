■Peeler status (n = 8, a = 3)
Bit 0: When the continuous issuing mode is selected, this bit is always 0.
Bit 2: When the peeling issuing mode is selected, this bit is changed during paper feeding or when a 
label is in the peeling position.  When the continuous issuing mode is selected, this bit is always 1.
The peeling issuing mode/continuous issuing mode is selected with the operation shown below.
