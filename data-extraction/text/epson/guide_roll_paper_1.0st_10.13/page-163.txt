Paper roll
Panel button command     ESC c 5
 
Ver. 10.13
p. 163
When the cover is open, or the roll paper end sensor detects a paper end, the FEED button is 
disabled regardless of the settings of this command. ("Cover" in the peeler specification indicates 
the peeler cover.)
TM-U230
The panel button is PAPER FEED.
When you change the roll paper after the printer stops printing because of roll paper end, the 
following processing is done regardless of the settings of this command.
•
When the printer is waiting for the roll paper to be set (the LED is off), the PAPER FEED is enabled, and 
paper can be fed.
•
When the LED blinks to indicate that the printer is confirming the status, the PAPER FEED is enabled. Even 
if the switch is pressed, paper cannot be fed.
TM-U220
The panel button is FEED.
When the roll paper end sensor detects a paper end, the FEED button is enabled regardless of the 
setting of this command.
When the cover is open, the FEED button is disabled regardless of the settings of this command.
