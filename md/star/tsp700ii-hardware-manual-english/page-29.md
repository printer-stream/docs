## 5-2. Unrecoverable errors 

Turn the printer off, check the recovery conditions, and then turn the printer back on. If the same error occurs after you turn the printer back on, the printer needs to be repaired.

Note 1:  If an unrecoverable error occurs, turn the power off immediately. 

- 2:  A power voltage error may be a result of a power supply malfunction. 

- 3:  If the paper is jammed, turn the power off, clear the jammed paper, and then turn the power back on. For details, see section 6-2, “Removing Paper Jams”. 

If you find an error other than the above, try turning the power off, and then on. If the same error is displayed even after the power is turned on again, it may be a printer malfunction. Contact the seller.
