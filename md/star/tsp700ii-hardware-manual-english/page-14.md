## **3-6. Loading a Paper Roll** 

Use a paper roll that complies with the printer specifications. 

- (1) Push the cover open lever, and open the printer cover.

- (2) If the thickness of the paper is 65 to 99 µm, install the tension bar on the printer as shown. If the thickness of the paper is 100 to 150 µm, it is not necessary to install the tension bar. 

**Note : A tension bar cannot be installed when a roll paper guide is installed in a printer.** 

- **Please remove the roll paper guide and then install the tension bar.**

- (4) After the tension bar is installed, please install the roll paper guide again.
