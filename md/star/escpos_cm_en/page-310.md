## **6-10  Appendix 10  Special Specifications when the Bluetooth Interface (IFBD-HB03) is used** 

## **6-10-1 Self-printing** 

1) When the Bluetooth information is printed at the end of self-printing, the communication with the host device is terminated. 

Therefore, if you continue to transmit data using commands after self-printing, you need to open the virtual COM port again.
