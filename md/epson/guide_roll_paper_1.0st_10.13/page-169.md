## **C O N F I D E N T I A L** 

## TM-U230 

**Bits 2 and 3 are undefined.** 

**The roll paper end sensor is always enabled, and when it detects a paper-end, the printer stops printing.** 

**When a roll paper near-end or a roll paper end is detected, the PAPER OUT LED comes on.** 

**When the built-in buzzer is enabled (DIP switch 2-8 is ON), the buzzer rings when the PAPER OUT LED comes on.** 

## TM-U220 

**Bits 2 and 3 are undefined.** 

**The roll paper end sensor is always enabled, and when it detects a paper-end, the printer stops printing.** 

**When a roll paper near-end or a roll paper end is detected, the PAPER OUT LED comes on.** 

**The roll paper near-end sensor is an option. When the roll paper near-end sensor is not included, this command is ignored.**
