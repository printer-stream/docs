## **C O N F I D E N T I A L** 

   - **For the sound patterns, see** ESC ( A **<Function 97>, Sound buzzer in** TM-T88V **models (registered sound pattern specified).** 

- **Buzzer function: Sound pattern (Autocut) (when** _**a**_ **= 121) (optional external buzzer) Buzzer function: Sound pattern (Pulse 1) (when** _**a**_ **= 123) (optional external buzzer) Buzzer function: Sound pattern (Pulse 2) (when** _**a**_ **= 125) (optional external buzzer)** 

   - **Pulse 1 and Pulse 2 are generated with** ESC p **: Generate pulse Pulse 1 when drawer kick-out connector pin 2 is selected. Pulse 2 when drawer kick-out connector pin 5 is selected.** 

   - **Sound patterns can be specified as below:** 

|**(**nL**+**nH ×**256)**|**Soundpattern**|
|---|---|
|**1**|**Pattern A**|
|**2**|**Pattern B**|
|**3**|**Pattern C**|
|**4**|**Pattern D**|
|**5**|**Pattern E**|



   - **For the sound patterns, see** ESC ( A **<Function 97>, Sound buzzer in** TM-T88V **models (registered sound pattern specified).** 

- **Buzzer function: Buzzer frequency (Autocut) (when** _**a**_ **= 122) (optional external buzzer) Buzzer function: Buzzer frequency (Pulse 1) (when** _**a**_ **= 124) (optional external buzzer) Buzzer function: Buzzer frequency (Pulse 2) (when** _**a**_ **= 126) (optional external buzzer)** 

   - **Buzzer frequency can be specified as below:** 

|**(**nL**+**nH ×**256)**|**Buzzer frequency**|
|---|---|
|**0**|**No sound**|
|**1**|**1 time**|



- **For “Buzzer frequency (Autocut)”, the buzzer sounds for cutting executed with a cutting command and cutting at cover close.**
