## **C O N F I D E N T I A L** 

## TM-P60 

**This function uses the “data quantity (** k **) + number of control information data” area for execution.** 

**When data quantity (** k **) is odd number: number of control information data is 16 bytes. When data quantity (** k **) is even number: number of control information data is 17 bytes.**
