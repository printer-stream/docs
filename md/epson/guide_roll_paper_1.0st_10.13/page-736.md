|---|---|
|**1**|**Pattern A**|
|**2**|**Pattern B**|
|**3**|**Pattern C**|

   - **For the sound patterns, see** ESC ( A **<Function 97>, Sound buzzer in** TM-T20 **models (registered sound pattern specified).** 

- **Buzzer function: Buzzer frequency (Autocut) (when a = 122) (optional external buzzer) Buzzer function: Buzzer frequency (Pulse 1) (when a = 124) (optional external buzzer) Buzzer function: Buzzer frequency (Pulse 2) (when a = 126) (optional external buzzer)**

|**(**nL**+**nH ×**256)**|**Buzzer frequency**|
|---|---|
