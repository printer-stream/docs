## _Removing Jammed Paper_ 

When a paper jam occurs, never pull out the paper forcibly. Open the roll paper cover and remove the jammed paper. 

When the roll paper cover cannot be opened, follow the steps below to remove the jammed 

paper. 

**Do not touch the thermal head** (See "Cleaning the Thermal Head/Platen Roller" on page 53.) **because it can be very hot after printing.** 

## CAUTION 

- 1 Turn off the printer. 

- 2 Slide the cutter cover toward the front to open it. 

**==> picture [177 x 167] intentionally omitted <==**

- 3 Turn the knob until you see a triangle in the opening. 

This returns the cutter blade to the normal position. There is a label near the cutter to assist you.
